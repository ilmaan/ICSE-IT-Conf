Travel and Venue

The 2nd International Conference on Software Engineering of Emerging Technologies (SEET-26) is scheduled to take place at Penn State University, Behrend, Erie, Pennsylvania, USA from August 10 to 11, 2026.
Erie is a city in Northwestern Pennsylvania located on the south shore of Lake Erie, known for its role as Pennsylvania's only Great Lakes port and its historical maritime connections, particularly the Battle of Lake Erie and Commodore Oliver Hazard Perry's flagship, the U.S.S. Niagara. The city features numerous attractions, including Presque Isle State Park, Waldameer Park & Water World, Splash Lagoon Indoor Water Park, Presque Isle boat tour, the Erie Art Museum, and a downtown cultural district. Numerous attractions can be found within a 70-90 mile radius, including Niagara Falls, the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, the Cleveland Museum of Art, West Side Market, and the Cleveland Botanical Garden.
